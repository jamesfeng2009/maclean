# 18 Pricing Strategy

## Competitive anchor
Developer cleaner competitors already use low-cost lifetime pricing. Therefore maclean should not win by being the cheapest.

## Recommended

### Founding Pro
$19.99 lifetime
- 1–3 Macs depending on launch policy
- all V1 Pro
- founding badge
- lifetime V1.x

### Standard Pro
$29.99 lifetime
- up to 3 Macs
- full Pro

### Team
$5/device/month
- minimum 5 devices
- central policy and fleet dashboard

## Price testing
A/B test:
A: $19.99 lifetime
B: $29.99 lifetime
Do not change the feature set during the test.

Measure:
- checkout initiation
- purchase conversion
- refund
- support load
- multi-Mac usage

## Upgrade
If a customer owns 1 Mac and later needs 3:
- pay a small upgrade difference
- never force repurchase

## Pricing page principles
- no fake crossed-out prices
- no countdown timers
- clear number of devices
- clear update policy
- clear refund policy
- clear distinction between lifetime license and Team subscription

## Why lifetime first
Desktop cleaners have episodic usage. Lifetime reduces purchase friction and aligns with the initial value proposition.

## Why Team subscription later
Team value is recurring:
- fleet inventory
- policy management
- compliance
- analytics
- support

## Guardrails
Never make basic safety features paid.
Never remove previously purchased core features because a new subscription tier launches.
