## GEO / answer-engine strategy

2. evidence/example

## Internal linking
Problem article → category guide → product capability → pricing.

## AEO answer format
Lead with:
> “If you are a developer, the safest first targets are regenerable artifacts such as Xcode DerivedData, node_modules and Docker build cache. Do not treat local AI models or project source as ordinary cache.”

Then explain evidence and exceptions.

## Content cadence
2 high-quality technical articles/week initially.
Prefer original measurements, screenshots and reproducible experiments over generic AI-written content.
